WeChat in English, beyond the menus

WeChat can show its menus in English, and you can long-press a single message to translate it. That still leaves a lot in Chinese: busy group chats, notices posted as images, official account articles, and mini programs for booking, ordering and paying.

What WeChat already does

  • English menus: Me → Settings → General → Language.
  • Translate one message: long-press the message and choose Translate.

What stays in Chinese

  • Group chats: building, school and work groups move fast, and translating one message at a time doesn't keep up.
  • Notices sent as images: water cuts, rules, schedules.
  • Official account articles.
  • Mini programs: restaurants' order-at-table pages, bookings, parking, hospital appointments.

Read a whole WeChat screen in one tap

With Hanzi, on any WeChat screen (a group chat, a notice image or a mini program), tap once (the floating on-screen button, or Back Tap) and the whole screen comes back in English on top of the app. Your chats are processed on the phone and never uploaded.

  1. Open the chat, article or mini program.
  2. Tap the floating Hanzi button (or the back of your iPhone twice, if you set up Back Tap).
  3. Read everything at once, including text inside images.

Tips for building and work groups

  • Pin the group and check it daily: notices about deliveries, repairs and payments arrive there first.
  • Translate the images too: the important notice is often a poster, not a message.
